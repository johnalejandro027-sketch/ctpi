Company History

Clio Tech Plastic Inc. (CTPI) took over operations of I-Tung Plastic Engineering Company (founded in 1989). We hold over 30 years of pioneer expertise in plastic injection mold fabrication across the Philippines.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To seamlessly blend precision mold manufacturing with efficient, sustainable, high-quality production.

Vision: Leading precision mold manufacturing while pioneering eco-friendly practices in the industry.
